Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Introduction

Piri ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our perimenopause tracking application.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and name (if provided via Sign in with Apple or email registration).

Health Data: You may choose to log symptoms, mood, sleep quality, energy levels, menstrual cycle information, and other wellness data. Your entries are stored on your device. Apple Health data that you choose to connect is read on your device only and is never uploaded.

Usage Data: We collect analytics about how the app is used (for example which screens are opened and whether a check-in was completed) to improve the app. During setup, the answers you give (such as your age range and stage) are included so we can understand which setup steps work. The contents of your check-ins (symptoms, mood and sleep values) are not included in analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To generate personalized insights and recommendations
  • To improve and develop new features
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service

Data Storage and Security

Your symptom entries are stored on your device. Account information (your email address and sign-in details) is stored with Firebase, a Google Cloud service, with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information.

Peri, the AI Assistant

Peri is an optional assistant inside the app. It is off until you turn it on.

When you ask Peri a question, or when Peri writes your weekly note, the app sends a summary of your recent entries (for example symptom days, mood, sleep, energy, stress, triggers and cycle day) together with your question to our servers, which pass it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. We do not send your name, email address or Apple Health data.

We do not store your questions or Peri's answers on our servers. We keep a count of how many questions each account has asked so we can apply usage limits. Anthropic processes the data on our behalf under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models, and may retain it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring.

Peri gives general wellness information. It is not a doctor and does not diagnose or treat. You can turn Peri off at any time in the Peri screen (the "..." menu).

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for research purposes. Your health data is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent; if you turn on Peri, the summary described above is processed by Anthropic to answer your questions.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Export your data (available in the app)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of analytics collection

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app, which will permanently remove your account data from our servers. Entries stored on your device are removed when you delete the app.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
