Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my symptoms?

Tap the "Track" tab at the bottom of the screen. You can log symptoms, mood, sleep quality, energy levels, and more. Quick log buttons make it easy to record common triggers like caffeine, alcohol, or stress.

How do I see my patterns and insights?

Go to the "Insights" tab to view your trends, calendar, cycle tracking, and AI-powered pattern analysis that shows correlations between your triggers and symptoms.

How do I export my data?

Go to Profile > Export Data. Premium users can export their data as PDF or CSV files to share with their healthcare provider.

How do I generate a doctor report?

Go to Profile > Generate Doctor Report. This creates a comprehensive PDF summary of your symptoms and patterns that you can share with your healthcare provider.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app. Go to Profile and scroll to the bottom to find the Sign Out option. To completely delete your data, please contact peritrackapp@gmail.com.

Is my health data secure?

Yes. Your entries are stored on your device, and your account information is stored securely with Firebase (Google Cloud). We never sell your personal health information. If you turn on Peri, the optional AI assistant, a summary of your recent entries is sent to our AI provider to answer your questions, as described in the Privacy Policy. Peri is off until you turn it on.
